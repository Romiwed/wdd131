Hometown Explorer

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Hometown Explorer

I chose this name because it clearly communicates that the website helps people explore interesting places in a local community. The name is short, memorable, and appropriate for a travel guide about my hometown.

Optional domain: hometownexplorer.org

Site Purpose

Hometown Explorer will help visitors and local residents discover places to visit, eat, shop, and learn in my hometown. The website will provide short descriptions of parks, restaurants, museums, historical landmarks, shopping areas, and other attractions.

It will also include photographs, practical visitor information, and a contact page. A JavaScript feature will allow users to save their favorite places.

Scenarios

  • What are the best attractions to visit during a one-day trip?
  • Where can I find a family-friendly park or museum?
  • How can I save my favorite places so I can review them later?
  • How can I contact the website owner with a question or recommendation?
